Explore Our Cliffs

West Cliff

The most popular and most visited cliff, located closest to the main entrance. Offers fantastic ocean views, hiking trails, and easy road access.

North Cliff

Known for family-friendly activities and calmer recreational areas, North Cliff is a favorite for day trips and picnics.

East Cliff

East Cliff features rugged terrain and treasure trail hikes, popular among more adventurous visitors.

South Cliff

South Cliff offers sunset camping spots and canyon exploring, making it ideal for overnight stays.
